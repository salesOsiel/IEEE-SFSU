Ways to get involved at SF State.

Engineering at SF State is bigger than any one chapter. This is a running directory of the student organizations and research labs on campus, so you know where to look before you go looking.

Student organizations

Clubs, teams, and honor societies.

Every engineering org recognized by the School of Engineering. Most are open to any major, and reaching out over email or Discord is usually enough to get started.

Source: SF State School of Engineering student organizations

Research labs

Faculty labs taking students.

Undergraduate research is one of the fastest ways to get hands-on experience. Email a lab director whose work interests you — that is genuinely how most students get in.
