Join IEEE at SFSU

Join locally by joining Discord and showing up.

To join IEEE at SFSU, you only need to join our Discord and attend chapter events. A paid national IEEE membership is not required unless you personally want the extra IEEE benefits.

Local chapter access

No subscription is needed to participate with us.

IEEE at SFSU is meant to be easy to enter. If you are in the Discord, receiving announcements, and attending meetings or events, you are part of the local chapter community.

National IEEE membership can still be valuable for students who want access to additional resources, publications, discounts, or conference opportunities, but it is optional for local chapter involvement.

Local chapter

Discord plus events

This is the main path for students who want meetings, workshops, project nights, socials, and community without extra barriers.

Optional upgrade

National IEEE membership

Choose this only if you want the broader IEEE network, member resources, publication access, or conference-related benefits.

How to join

A simple path from curious to involved.

New students do not need prior experience, a project idea, or a paid IEEE subscription. Start with Discord, then come meet people.
